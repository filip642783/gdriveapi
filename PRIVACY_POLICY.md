**Effective Date:** November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how rclone ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our application to interact with Google Drive services.

---

### 1. Information We Access and Collect

When you authenticate with Google Drive through our application, we may request access to your Google account and Google Drive data:

- **Google Account Profile:** Basic account information (such as your email address and profile name) to authenticate your identity.
- **Google Drive Files and Metadata:** Depending on the permissions you grant:
  - File metadata (file names, IDs, MIME types, file sizes, creation/modification dates).
  - File contents (only files created, opened, or specifically selected by you through the application).

We do **not** access, read, or modify any files outside the explicit scope and permissions granted by you during authorization.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data accessed from Google Drive strictly to provide the core functionality of the application, including:

- Connecting to your Google Drive account.
- Reading, creating, updating, downloading, or syncing the files you select.
- Displaying your files within the application interface.

We do **not** use your Google Drive data for:
- Advertising or marketing purposes.
- Training machine learning or artificial intelligence models without your explicit consent.
- Selling, renting, or leasing your personal information to third parties.
